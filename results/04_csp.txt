--- DOES RESTRICTING THE ELECTRODES HELP? ---
  A all 64                 : 71.3 % +- 5.7
  B sensorimotor strip     : 62.4 % +- 4.7  (-8.9 pts vs A)
  C all 64 + shrinkage     : 71.6 % +- 5.8  (+0.2 pts vs A)

  Rule: a gap smaller than the combined spread (7.4 pts)
  cannot be called a difference at this sample size.
  -> A beats B by 8.9 pts, above the spread. The extra
     electrodes carry real information the motor strip does not.

  Shrinkage changes nothing at all. Worth knowing why: it rescales the
  CSP filters but leaves the spatial PATTERNS almost untouched (max
  difference ~4e-05 here). The covariance estimate is therefore not
  the bottleneck for this subject — the data itself is.

--- SYNTHESIS: WHAT STEP 5 ACTUALLY ESTABLISHED ---
  1. Motor imagery IS decodable from the motor strip alone:
     62.4 % vs a 51.1 % majority baseline. Its CSP
     patterns peak at CP5, C4, FC3 and C3 — over the sensorimotor cortex,
     where physiology says they should be. This is the clean result.

  2. Using all 64 electrodes scores HIGHER (71.3 %), but its top
     components are not motor: component 1 puts 12 % of its
     weight on the motor strip and peaks at O1, an occipital electrode.
     Component 2 peaks at P3. Both are parietal/occipital.

  3. So step 4's red flag is CONFIRMED — and in the most treacherous way
     possible. The non-motor signal does not degrade accuracy, it
     IMPROVES it by 8.9 points. A project that only looked at the
     score would report 71.3 % and claim to have decoded motor
     imagery. The patterns are what expose it.

  4. What we still do not know: WHAT that posterior signal is. Step 4
     rejected the visual cue (it is bilateral), a single bad trial, and
     fatigue. It is reproducible enough to survive 50 train/test splits,
     so calling it pure noise no longer fits either — noise does not
     generalise. That is an open question, stated as open.

  PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE for step 6 and 7: report both variants side by
  side, and treat variant B as the honest motor-imagery result even
  though it is the lower number. The headline accuracy of this project
  should be the one we can defend, not the one that looks best.
